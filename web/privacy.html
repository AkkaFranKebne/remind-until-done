Privacy

rud. is a private, invite-only test app. Ewa runs it as a private person, not a company, and it sells nothing. This page says what rud. keeps about you, why, and for how long.

What rud. keeps

  • Your email and a sign-in ID

    To sign you in and know which reminders are yours. Google sign-in also keeps your Google name.

    Kept until you delete your account.

  • Your settings

    Time zone, default nag interval and default time.

    Kept until you delete your account.

  • Your reminders

    Title, note and times. That’s what rud. nags you about.

    Deleted 180 days after you mark one done.

  • Your device’s push address

    Plus the device type and browser name. So pushes reach that device.

    Removed when you sign out, when the device stops taking pushes, or after 270 days unused with nothing left to send.

  • Each reminder’s history

    When it was made, sent and done. So Ewa can find out why a push didn’t arrive.

    Deleted after 90 days.

  • The nag lines you’ve seen

    So you don’t get the same joke twice.

    Kept until you delete your account.

  • Server logs

    Google keeps a technical record of each request, such as the IP address and browser. It never includes your reminders or email.

    Deleted after 30 days.

Your browser keeps you signed in on this device. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Just nagging.

Who handles it

  • Google

    Sign-in, the website, the server and its logs. The server and logs are in Frankfurt. Sign-in data can be stored outside the EU.

  • Neon

    The database, in Frankfurt.

  • Your browser’s push service

    Pushes travel through the service of whoever makes your browser or device. Google for Android and Chrome, Apple for iPhone and iPad, Mozilla for Firefox, Microsoft for Edge.

Your choices

  • Delete your account

    In Settings, tap Delete account. Your account, reminders, history and device go right away. Server logs disappear on their own after 30 days.

  • Turn pushes off

    In your device’s settings, or sign out of rud.

  • Get a copy of your data, or ask something

    Ask Ewa, who invited you.
